# Meeting summary

The team lead runs this after a meeting and gets a one-page summary in chat: the decisions, the owners, and the open questions.

## Step 1. Read the transcript

Read the transcript file the lead names.

**Done when:** you can list every decision with its owner.

## Step 2. Write the summary

Write the decisions, the owners and the open questions, one line each.

**Done when:** the summary is shown in chat.
