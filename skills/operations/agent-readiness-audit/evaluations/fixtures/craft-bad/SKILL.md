# Weekly ticket summary

The support lead runs this once a week and gets a one-page summary of the ticket export.

## Step 1. Read the export

Read the export file the lead names and count the tickets by status.

Keep the weekly summary short enough for the lead to read in two minutes.

## Step 2. Write the summary

Write the counts and the three oldest open tickets. Read references/rules.md before writing.
